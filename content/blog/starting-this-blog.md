I've rebuilt this portfolio a few times, but this is the first version with
a blog attached to it. The idea is simple: write short posts about what I'm
building, what broke, and what I learned fixing it.

## Why bother

Most of what I learn happens while debugging something at 1am, not while
reading documentation. Writing it down after the fact is the only way I
actually retain it.

## What to expect

- Build logs for bigger projects (habit trackers, study tools, whatever's next)
- Notes on tools and patterns I end up reaching for repeatedly
- Occasional opinions about software that nobody asked for

New posts just need a markdown file dropped into `content/blog/` — no CMS,
no database, just files in the repo.
